# Cole Dorazio 🏍️

I’m a designer, engineer, and software developer focused on building useful digital experiences and solving practical problems. My work spans web development, application development, automation, data, and AI-assisted workflows. I’m currently studying Computer Science with a focus in Artificial Intelligence.

I’m also the founder of [Cyberflight Studios](#cyberflight-studios), a digital media business focused on photography, videography, drone work, and online content. Alongside software and business projects, I explore [3D design and printing](#3d-design-and-printing) as hands-on design and engineering work.

I care about clear solutions, thoughtful design, and responsible use of technology. I enjoy collaborating with others, learning new tools, and taking ideas from concept to a polished result.
